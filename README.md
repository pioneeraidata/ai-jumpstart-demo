# AI Jumpstart Demo Vault (Rivertown Heating & Air)

A Pioneer AI & Data demonstration vault. It shows a prospect exactly what an AI Jumpstart builds: a permanent, portable "context layer" that makes any AI tool behave like it works for their business. Rivertown Heating & Air is a fictional HVAC company, so there is no real personal or client information in here.

## How to use it in a sales call or group session

1. Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian. The Pioneer brand colors and folder lanes load automatically.
2. Walk the prospect through `Start Here.md`, then open `AGENTS.md` and say "this one file is what keeps you in control of the AI."
3. Show the core context in `00 - Business Context/` (About the Business, Our Voice & Tone, Services & Pricing).
4. The money moment: open `40 - Prompt Pack/AI Prompt Pack.md`, copy a prompt into Claude or ChatGPT with the vault loaded, and let them watch it answer in the business's voice with the real prices. That is the "oh, I get it" moment.
5. Close with `About the AI Jumpstart.md`, which carries the pitch and the $197 offer.

## Why HVAC

The talking points work well with trades (HVAC, roofing, plumbing) and local service businesses. Swap Rivertown for any industry by rewriting the six notes in `00 - Business Context/`; the structure stays the same.

## Structure

```
Start Here.md              What this is, how to use it in any AI tool
AGENTS.md                  The rules any AI follows (make AI behave)
About the AI Jumpstart.md  The branded pitch + $197 offer
00 - Business Context/     The core: identity, voice, pricing, customers, FAQs
10 - Customers/            A customer profile so AI has the history
20 - How We Work (SOPs)/   Quoting, scheduling, follow-up
30 - Marketing/            Brand, email templates, social posts
40 - Prompt Pack/          Copy-paste prompts that know the business
90 - System/               How it works + the Business MOC
```

Follows the Pioneer vault standards: no em dashes, plain English, Connected Notes footers, brand lanes. Built by Pioneer AI & Data.
