# 🔥 Rivertown Heating & Air: Your AI Context Layer

This is the thing that makes AI finally behave for your business.

Every time you open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, it has never met you. It does not know your business, your prices, your voice, or how you handle a customer. So you spend the first ten minutes catching it up, and it is still guessing. This vault fixes that. It is your business, written down once, in plain language, so any AI tool can read it and act like it actually works for you.

One setup. Permanent context. Works in every tool you will ever use.

## How to use it (30 seconds)

1. Open the note that fits what you are doing (a quote follow-up, a slow-season email, a tough customer reply).
2. Copy the relevant context notes, or load this whole folder into a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Project, or a Gemini Gem once.
3. Ask for what you need. Now the AI writes like Rivertown, not like a robot.
