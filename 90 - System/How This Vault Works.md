# ⚙️ How This Vault Works

The whole thing in one page, so anyone at Rivertown can keep it useful.

## The idea

This vault is your business, written down once, so any AI tool can read it and act like it works here. That is the context layer. You built it in the Jumpstart session, and it works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and whatever comes next.

## The parts

- **[AGENTS](../AGENTS.md):** the rulebook. Any AI reads this first and behaves: your voice, a human approves, never make up a price.
- **00 - Business Context:** the core. Who you are, how you sound, what you sell, who you serve.
- **10 - Customers:** a note per customer so AI has the history.
- **20 - How We Work:** your quoting, scheduling, and follow-up.
- **30 - Marketing:** brand, emails, and social in your voice.
- **40 - Prompt Pack:** copy-paste prompts that already know your business.

## Keeping it good

- When a price or policy changes, update the note. The AI is only as current as this vault.
- Add a customer note when someone becomes a regular.
- If AI ever writes something that does not sound like you, the fix is usually one line added to [Our Voice & Tone](../00%20-%20Business%20Context/Our%20Voice%20%26%20Tone.md) or [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md).
