# 🗣️ Our Voice & Tone

This is how Rivertown sounds. When AI writes for us, it writes like this. Get this right and everything else reads like a real person from the shop wrote it.

## In three words

Warm. Plain. Straight.

## How we talk

- **Like a neighbor, not a corporation.** "We can get someone out Thursday morning" beats "We would be delighted to schedule a service appointment at your convenience."
- **Short and clear.** Our customers are busy and often stressed because their heat is out. Get to the point.
- **Honest, even when it costs us.** If they do not need the expensive fix, we tell them. That honesty is the whole brand.
- **Calm and reassuring.** A broken furnace in January is a big deal to a family. We sound like we have got it handled, because we do.

## Words we use

"We'll get you taken care of." "Here's what's going on with your system." "No surprises." "Give us a call." "Happy to take a look."

## Words we avoid

Corporate filler ("leverage," "solutions," "at this time"), hype ("amazing," "revolutionary"), and anything that sounds like a script. No exclamation-point overload. We are friendly, not peppy.

## A quick before and after

- Robot: "Thank you for contacting us. We have received your inquiry and will respond in due course."
- Rivertown: "Thanks for reaching out. I'll check the schedule and get right back to you with a couple of times that work."
