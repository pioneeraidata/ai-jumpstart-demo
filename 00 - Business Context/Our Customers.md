# 👥 Our Customers

Who we serve, and what they actually care about. This helps AI write things that land, because it knows who is on the other end.

## Who they are

Homeowners in and around Rivertown, mostly 35 to 70. Families, retirees, and a lot of repeat customers whose parents used us too. Not looking for the cheapest, looking for someone they can trust in their house.

## What they care about

- **Their family being comfortable.** Heat out in January with a newborn home is an emergency, and we treat it like one.
- **Not getting ripped off.** They have been burned by a big outfit that oversold them. Straight talk is why they call us.
- **Someone reliable.** They want to know we will actually show up when we say.

## What they worry about

- A tech pushing a $6,000 unit when a $300 repair would do.
- Being stuck without heat or AC for days.
- Hidden fees and surprise charges.

## How to write for them

Reassure first, then be clear and honest. No pressure, no jargon. Remember they are often stressed when they contact us, so lead with "we've got you."
