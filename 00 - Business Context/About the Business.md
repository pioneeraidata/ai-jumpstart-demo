# 🏠 About Rivertown Heating & Air

The core of the whole context layer. If an AI reads only one note, it reads this one.

## The basics

- **What we do:** Residential heating and cooling. Install, repair, and maintenance for furnaces, AC units, heat pumps, and water heaters.
- **Where:** Rivertown and the surrounding county. About a 30 minute radius.
- **Who we are:** Family-run since 2009. Owner Mike Dolan, plus three techs and one office manager.
- **Hours:** Mon to Fri, 7:30 to 5:00. Emergency service on nights and weekends for existing customers.

## What we believe

We show up when we say we will, we tell people the truth about their system, and we do not sell anyone a new unit they do not need. Most of our work comes from word of mouth, which only happens if you treat people right the first time.

## What makes us different

- We give straight answers. If a repair buys you two more years, we say so instead of pushing a replacement.
- We show up on time and clean up after ourselves.
- The owner still runs calls. You are not getting a call center.

## Busy season and slow season

- **Busy:** first heat wave of summer, first cold snap of fall. Phones do not stop.
- **Slow:** spring and fall shoulder months. This is when we run maintenance promos and follow up with past customers.

## The stuff we want AI to help with

Quote follow-ups, replies to customer questions, slow-season emails, review requests, and social posts. All in our voice, none of it sent without Mike's ok.
