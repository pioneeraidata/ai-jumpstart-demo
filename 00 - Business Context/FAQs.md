# ❓ FAQs

The questions customers ask over and over, with our real answers. AI uses these so replies are consistent and correct, in our voice.

**How much is a service call?**
$89, and we waive it if you book the repair with us. See [Services & Pricing](Services%20%26%20Pricing.md).

**How fast can you come out?**
Usually within a day or two. For an existing customer with no heat or AC, we push to same day.

**Do you charge for a quote on a new system?**
No. Installs are quoted free, in person, so we can see your actual setup.

**My furnace is old. Should I repair or replace it?**
Depends on the unit and the cost of the fix. We will give you the honest math and let you decide. We do not push replacements you do not need.

**Do you offer financing?**
Yes, on installs. We can walk you through the options when we quote.

**Are you licensed and insured?**
Yes, fully. Happy to send proof.

**Do you service my area?**
If you are within about 30 minutes of Rivertown, yes. If you are not sure, just ask.
