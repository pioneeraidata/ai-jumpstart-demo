# 📝 How We Quote

The way Rivertown quotes work. AI uses this to draft quotes and follow-ups that match how we actually do it.

## Repairs

1. Diagnose on site. $89 service call, waived if they book the repair.
2. Give the price before we start. No surprises, ever.
3. If it is a repair-or-replace call, lay out both honestly and let them decide.

## Installs

1. Always quoted in person. We need to see the setup.
2. Give a ballpark range on the phone if they push, using [Services & Pricing](../00%20-%20Business%20Context/Services%20%26%20Pricing.md), and say the firm number comes after we see it.
3. Written quote same day or next morning while it is fresh.
4. Mention financing on anything over $2,500.

## The rule

Every quote is honest, clear, and has no hidden fees. If a cheaper option would serve them, we tell them about it. That is why they call us back.
