# 🗓️ Scheduling & Dispatch

How we run the calendar. Helps AI answer "how soon can you come out" and draft scheduling messages correctly.

## The basics

- Standard jobs booked within a day or two.
- Existing customers with no heat or AC get pushed to same day when we can.
- Three techs. Mornings are for installs, afternoons for service calls, roughly.
- We call or text a heads-up before a tech heads over. Some customers, like Sharon Henderson, really want that.

## When we are slammed (busy season)

- Triage: no heat or no AC first, especially older customers and families with little kids.
- If we cannot make it fast, we say so honestly and give a real window instead of overpromising.

## Drafting scheduling messages

Confirm the day and a rough window, promise a heads-up before arrival, and keep it short. Never commit to an exact minute we cannot hit.
