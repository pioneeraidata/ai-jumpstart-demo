# 🔁 Follow-Up Playbook

Most jobs are won or lost in the follow-up, and it is the thing that slips when we are slammed. This is where AI earns its keep: draft the follow-up, we approve and send.

## Quote follow-up

- **Day 2 after a quote:** short, friendly check-in. "Wanted to make sure you got the quote and see if any questions came up."
- **Day 7:** one more nudge, no pressure. Offer to hop on a quick call.
- Stop after two. We do not badger people.

## After a completed job

- **Next day:** thank them, confirm everything is working, ask them to call if anything feels off.
- **Day 3:** if they were happy, ask for a review. See the review request in [Email Templates](../30%20-%20Marketing/Email%20Templates.md).

## Maintenance customers

- **Seasonal:** reach out before summer and before winter to book the tune-up.
- **Comfort Club:** remind them their visits are included, so they use them.

## Voice

Every follow-up sounds like a person from the shop, not a marketing blast. Warm, short, no pressure. See [Our Voice & Tone](../00%20-%20Business%20Context/Our%20Voice%20%26%20Tone.md).
