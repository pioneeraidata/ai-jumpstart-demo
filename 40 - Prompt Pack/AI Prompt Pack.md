# ⚡ AI Prompt Pack

The fastest way to feel what the context layer does. Copy a prompt, run it in Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini with this vault loaded as context, and watch it answer like it works here.

> How to load the context: either paste the relevant notes into the chat, or set this folder up once as a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Project, or a Gemini Gem. Then these prompts just work.

## Quote follow-up

```
Using our voice, our pricing, and the customer note for [customer name], draft a day-2 follow-up email for the quote we sent on [job]. Keep it short and no-pressure. Do not invent any numbers that are not in Services & Pricing. Give it to me as a draft for Mike to approve.
```

## Answer a customer question

```
A customer asked: "[paste their question]." Draft a reply in the Rivertown voice using our FAQs and pricing. If the answer isn't in our notes, tell me what to confirm instead of guessing.
```

## Repair-or-replace explainer

```
Write a short, honest repair-vs-replace explanation for [customer], whose [system and age] needs [the issue]. Lay out both options plainly, no pressure, the way we actually talk. Pull history from their customer note.
```

## Slow-season email

```
Draft a spring maintenance email to our Comfort Club members reminding them to book their tune-up before summer. Warm, short, in our voice. A draft for Mike to review.
```

## Social post

```
Write 3 short Facebook posts for the first cold snap of the season. Honest, useful, neighborly, no hype. One tip, one heads-up, one behind-the-scenes.
```

## Review request

```
Draft a review request for [customer] who was happy with [job] two days ago. Short, genuine, includes our review link. Our voice.
```

## The rule baked into all of them

Every draft is for a human to approve. Nothing goes to a customer without Mike's ok, and no price or policy gets invented. That is set in [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md).
