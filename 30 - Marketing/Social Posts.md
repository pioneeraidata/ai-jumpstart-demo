# 📱 Social Posts

Simple, honest posts in our voice. No hype, no stock-photo energy. AI drafts these from real seasonal moments, Mike approves and posts.

## The kinds of posts that work for us

- **Seasonal heads-up:** "First cold snap is coming. If your furnace made a weird noise last winter, now is the time, not the night it quits."
- **Honest tip:** "You can add a year or two to your AC just by changing the filter every 90 days. Costs a few bucks. We will still be here when you do need us."
- **Behind the scenes:** a tech on a clean install, a quick note about a job done right.
- **Thank you / review:** sharing a kind word from a customer (with their ok).

## Voice reminder

Sound like a neighbor giving good advice, not a company running an ad. Short. Useful. Real. See [Our Voice & Tone](../00%20-%20Business%20Context/Our%20Voice%20%26%20Tone.md).
