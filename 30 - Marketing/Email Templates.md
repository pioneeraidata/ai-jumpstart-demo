# ✉️ Email Templates

Starting points in the Rivertown voice. AI adapts these to the specific customer using the customer note and the SOPs. Always approved by Mike before sending.

## Quote follow-up (day 2)

> Subject: Quick check-in on your quote
>
> Hi [name], just wanted to make sure the quote for [job] came through ok. Happy to walk through any of it or answer questions, no pressure at all. Give me a call or reply here whenever works. Thanks again for thinking of us.

## After a completed job

> Subject: All set, and thank you
>
> Hi [name], thanks for having us out today. Your [system] should be running great now. If anything feels off, call us right away and we will make it right. We appreciate your business.

## Review request (day 3, happy customer)

> Subject: Would you mind sharing how it went?
>
> Hi [name], glad we could get you taken care of. If you have a minute, a quick review really helps a small shop like ours: [link]. Either way, thanks for trusting us with it.

## Slow-season maintenance nudge

> Subject: Time for your [season] tune-up?
>
> Hi [name], before the [heat / cold] hits, it is a good time to get your [system] looked at so it does not quit on you at the worst moment. Want me to find you a spot on the calendar?
