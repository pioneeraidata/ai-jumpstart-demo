# 🎨 Brand at a Glance

The short version of who Rivertown is to the outside world. AI uses this so marketing sounds like us.

## The promise

Honest heating and cooling from people who treat your house like their own.

## The tagline

"Straight answers. Reliable service. No surprises."

## What we sound like

Warm, plain, straight. A neighbor who happens to be great at HVAC. See [Our Voice & Tone](../00%20-%20Business%20Context/Our%20Voice%20%26%20Tone.md).

## What we never do

Overpromise, oversell, or talk down to people. No fear tactics, no fake urgency, no jargon.

## Where we show up

Word of mouth first. Then a simple website, a Google Business profile we keep full of real reviews, and the occasional seasonal email and Facebook post.
